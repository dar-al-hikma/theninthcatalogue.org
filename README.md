# The Ninth Catalogue

The publisher's site, served at **<https://theninthcatalogue.org>**.

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| [The front door](https://theninthcatalogue.org/) | The catalogue's three entries: Dār al-Ḥikma, the collection; the Tower, the instrument; and Zīj, the application |
| [The Tower](https://theninthcatalogue.org/tower/) | The sky over any place, at any moment from 501 BC to AD 2200, in the browser; its sources and licences are under "Sources and notices" on the page |
| [Zīj](https://theninthcatalogue.org/zij/) | What Zīj is, and its downloads for Windows and macOS, with Linux from source |
| [Dār al-Ḥikma](https://theninthcatalogue.org/dar-al-hikma/) | A forwarding link to the collection at <https://dar-al-hikma.github.io>; `/dar-al-hikma/<piece>/` leads to that piece |

Each page is one HTML file and asks for nothing beyond this site. All but the Tower carry their
fonts inside them; the typefaces and their licences are listed in [FONT-LICENSES.md](FONT-LICENSES.md).
The Tower sets its text in the reader's system faces, and loads its places and clocks from its own
folder, `tower/places/`.

Dār al-Ḥikma is at <https://dar-al-hikma.github.io>. Zīj's source and releases are at
<https://github.com/dar-al-hikma/Zij>.

Correspondence: curator@theninthcatalogue.org
